About us

Learning with purpose, innovation, and impact.

Wangari Maathai University of Technology is inspired by a legacy of environmental stewardship, academic excellence, and community transformation.

The Wangari Maathai University of Technology (WaMUT) was founded to honor the legacy of Professor Wangari Maathai, a renowned Kenyan environmentalist, activist, and Nobel Peace Prize laureate. The university brings together technology, sustainability, and social responsibility in one powerful learning experience.

Our mission is to shape leaders who can solve real-world problems through innovation, ecological thinking, and ethical engineering. By combining rigorous academics with practical training, we prepare students to thrive in a fast-changing digital and global economy.
